Questions, Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

How many photographers and videographers cover our wedding?+

Team size depends on your package and number of functions — typically 2 to 4 members including a lead photographer, cinematographer and drone operator, ensuring both families and every angle are covered.

Do you travel outside Dehradun and Gurugram?+

Yes — we regularly shoot destination weddings across India and abroad. Travel, stay and logistics are built into your custom quote.

How soon do we receive our photos and films?+

A sneak-peek gallery and same-day highlight reel are ready within hours of your event. Fully edited photographs and the final cinematic film are typically delivered within 3–5 weeks.

Do you offer both candid and traditional photography?+

Always. Every package blends candid, emotion-led storytelling with complete traditional ritual documentation so both your memories and your family's archive are covered.

How do we lock our wedding date with Insidee Moments?+

Message us on WhatsApp with your date and city — we'll confirm availability, walk you through packages, and a small advance secures your date exclusively.
