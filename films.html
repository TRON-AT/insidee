// 08 — FAQ

Common questions before you brief us.

How much does a manufacturing film cost?+

It depends on shoot days, locations, crew size and post-production scope. Tell us about your plant and goals on the form below and we'll send a tailored quote within 24–48 hours.

Will filming disrupt our production line?+

We plan shoots around your production schedule and shift patterns, and our crews are briefed on factory safety protocols before we ever step on the floor.

Can you sign an NDA before the shoot?+

Yes — we're happy to sign your NDA or ours before discussing proprietary processes, layouts or formulations.

How long does a project take, start to finish?+

Most manufacturing films go from kickoff to final delivery in 3–6 weeks, depending on shoot complexity and revision rounds.

Do we own the final footage and film?+

Yes — usage rights and ownership are defined clearly in our agreement before the shoot begins.
