Questions

Common questions from prospective partners

Do I need a physical office?

No. A professional client-facing setup is recommended as the market matures, but a large office is not required to begin.

Do I need marketing experience?

Not necessarily. Sales and business-development ability are more important. Insidee provides service and solution training.

Who owns the client relationship?

You're the primary local relationship owner. Insidee HQ remains responsible for delivery standards and specialist execution.

Can I sell all Insidee services?

Yes, subject to service availability, pricing and capability.

Is revenue guaranteed?

No. Territory rights and support do not guarantee sales, clients or profitability.

Can I build my own team?

Yes. As you grow, local sales and account-management capability can be expanded, subject to Insidee's brand and quality standards.

Can I work from home initially?

Yes. The model is designed to be asset-light.

Can I have an exclusive territory?

Potentially, subject to territory availability and performance requirements.
